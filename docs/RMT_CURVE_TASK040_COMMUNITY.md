## Reviewer independence and deliverable

1. Ask each prospective reviewer for examples relevant to v4 settlement, reserves and migration. Record the reviewer identity or established public handle, relevant work, dates, methods, frozen commit and covered contracts.
2. Have the reviewer disclose authorship, prior involvement, current or prospective employment, competitive relationships, equity/token/fee/revenue interests, signer or operator roles, dependencies on sponsor compensation, and relationships with other reviewers. A design author or launch beneficiary may provide context. Use conflict-free reviewers for independent disposition.
3. Agree to $0 in fees, deposits, gifts, tokens, equity or bounties. Confirm private reporting, access limited to the approved package, retention, subcontractor/tool sharing, and any publication terms. Keep production keys, RPC credentials, backups and private infrastructure out of the scope. Use only synthetic fixtures or authorized public reads.
4. Ask for a signed or attributable scope and results report for the exact source and 4663 artifact. Request a per-finding disposition, reproducer or rationale, residual risks, coverage gaps and a documented retest if source changes. Preserve findings until the corresponding evidence is reviewed.
