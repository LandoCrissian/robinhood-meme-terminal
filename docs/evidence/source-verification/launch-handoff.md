## Security decision

The frozen scanner gate remains failed. The current register binds **18 engine highs, four dependency highs and seven governance medium findings** to internal pinned-path dispositions. An external audit is optional under the owner's policy. Stage-specific risk acceptance must identify the exact candidate, findings and residual risks. Confirmed exploitable high-impact defects and unresolved plausible material loss paths stop the affected stage. Funding, harvest and migration depend on the pinned runtime graph and immutable controls. [Current register](../fixed-supply-security/task063-findings.json) · [Machine policy](../production-readiness/task063-release-policy.json)

The new local regression tests a beneficiary callback during a combined ETH/token LP claim with two funded pools. The same-ledger nested claim reverted. The same-pool nested fee harvest rolled back and remained retryable. An unrelated pool settled to its own beneficiaries. Both original positions retained exact principal, ownership and approval state. The test preserves the open callback assumptions in the Slither register. [Callback regression](../../../packages/contracts/test/RMTV4SharedPoolSettlement.t.sol)
