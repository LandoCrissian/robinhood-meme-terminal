## Reciprocal support and accounting

Use an ordinary, independently approved treasury expense in each direction. The RMT Operations Safe can spend only its own settled revenue after verifying the real beneficiary and a project-specific authorization. Keep the RMT 90% project entitlement and 5% Reserve allocation segregated. LAMMB's authorized spend treasury can separately pay a documented RMT expense from its own settled funds. The royalty receiver and collection owner are observable on-chain roles. Any assignment of a LAMMB spending treasury depends on separate owner authority. Preserve the collection's payout settings and track royalty income independently of both projects' expenses.

For each transfer, record the chain, asset and decimals, gross amount, gas, purpose, approved budget, payee, approving authority, transaction hash, confirmed settlement and L1 finality status. Reconcile market-order consideration and the asset transfer before booking a royalty. Use the venue and receipt to attribute marketplace fees separately, and show any separately authorized treasury expense as an expense. OpenSea's terms prohibit price and metrics manipulation. Treasury support is accounted as an expense and stays outside trading activity. Any legal entity, tax treatment or related-party characterization depends on the operators' actual agreements and jurisdiction and remains unverified. Keep supporting approval and receipt records for that review. [OpenSea Platform Terms §3](https://opensea.io/tos)

**One remaining LAMMB input for any future live support payment.** Provide the LAMMB spending treasury's public chain and address with an authority reference and a specific budget and purpose. The verified collection for this document is LAMMB Genesis. If the intended collection differs, include its exact chain and address and an official deployment or repository reference in that same handoff.
