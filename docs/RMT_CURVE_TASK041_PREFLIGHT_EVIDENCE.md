## Wallet claims and acceptance

Fee beneficiaries are fixed in the factory and ledgers. Creation-fee release can be triggered by any caller but always attempts native ETH to operations. Curve `claim()` uses `msg.sender` as the fee beneficiary. The LP ledger sends native ETH and the launch ERC-20 to the claimant. If native receipt reverts, the combined claim rolls back, and LP `claimAsset(false)` lets that same beneficiary claim its token credit separately. The operating and separate reserve addresses must be checked for both receiving and initiating the paths that actually apply to them.
