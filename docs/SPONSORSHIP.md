# RMT sponsorship

GitHub Sponsors is a voluntary way to support the continued development of
Robinhood Meme Terminal (RMT), an independent, non-custodial discovery and
trading terminal for Robinhood Chain.

Sponsor support helps fund work that benefits the public product:

- production infrastructure and monitoring;
- security testing and independent-review preparation;
- verified Robinhood Chain, DEX, and launchpad integrations;
- mobile accessibility, performance, and reliability;
- public technical documentation and integration research; and
- maintenance of reusable or open-source components released by RMT.

## Sponsorship is not an investment

A sponsorship is voluntary support for development. It is not:

- an investment, loan, deposit, or purchase of an RMT token;
- a promise of profit, yield, revenue sharing, equity, or repayment;
- payment for a token listing, ranking, endorsement, or favorable risk label;
- permission to bypass RMT security, review, or consent requirements; or
- evidence of a partnership with RMT, Robinhood, Sushi, Pons, or another
  integrated project.

Sponsors do not receive control over user funds, contracts, treasury keys,
rankings, risk classifications, or product-governance decisions.

## Recognition and privacy

Recognition is optional. RMT will not publish a sponsor's name, logo, wallet,
or project without that sponsor's permission. Sponsorship does not require a
wallet address and should never involve sharing a private key or seed phrase.

## Transparency

The live product, protocol status, limitations, and material release evidence
remain publicly available:

- [RMT](https://www.rmtlaunch.fun/)
- [Protocol status](https://www.rmtlaunch.fun/status)
- [Risk disclosures](https://www.rmtlaunch.fun/risks)
- [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/LandoCrissian)

RMT is independent software and is not Robinhood Markets, Inc. Sponsorship
supports continued development; it does not make a sponsor an operator,
partner, or representative of RMT.
