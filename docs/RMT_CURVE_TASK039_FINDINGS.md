## Position funding (rows 1–8)

The path is permissionless threshold graduation through the locked curve, followed by the locked graduation engine and its private `_fundPosition` function. The engine checks the caller against the factory's active same-token curve, verifies dependency runtime identities, and constructs the canonical native/token PoolKey. A creator cannot supply a substitute token or callback contract. Pool initialization and position reservation roll back if funding fails. ## Curve graduation (rows 9–11)

## Same-token asset migration (rows 13–18)

The registry consumes the scheduled authorization before calling the non-reentrant factory. The public pointers update after verification, with EVM rollback on any failure. ## Cross-cutting acceptance for the reviewer

Confirm the exact compiler/dependency/deployment identity before relying on fixed-token, guarded initialization, Permit2, PoolManager or PositionManager assumptions. Analyze an already initialized or wrong-key pool as a failure and confirm the buy/graduation rollback cannot leave backing stranded. Review CREATE2 creator/intent separation and registry admission. Confirm the 31337 creation gate and the isolated review substitution for 4663 before discussing production permissioning. The owner must separately approve deployment, signer identities and independent operation. Any two of the three immutable signers can suspend the directory immediately.
