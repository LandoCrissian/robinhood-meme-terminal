# RMT canonical ecosystem flywheel

**Status: CURRENT OWNER PRODUCT AUTHORITY**

RMT is the market operating layer for Robinhood Chain. Its canonical product flywheel is:

```text
MARKETS
→ ACTIVITY
→ RMT
→ DISTRIBUTION
→ COMMUNITIES
→ MARKETS
```

The Token Terminal must first become useful and trade-worthy. Platform fees, buybacks, and Distribution economics are not prerequisites for completing that product.

## Current product decisions

- RMT connects Token markets, NFT markets, communities, and future Distribution while preserving the independent authority of each domain.
- CCFF00 is the first Distribution Center community. Its deterministic ERC-6551 token-bound wallets are intended future distribution endpoints; this does not make CCFF00 the generic Distribution architecture.
- Additional independently admitted communities may participate later.
- Broad Robinhood Chain Token market visibility is desired. Visibility is discovery and market-data presentation; it does not imply RMT endorsement, curated authority, execution eligibility, Project Market identity, or Distribution eligibility.
- The eight reviewed Token markets remain the canonical curated seed set. They are not the maximum public catalog.
- `ACTIVE` remains ahead of `TRENDING`. Membership in `ACTIVE`, `TRENDING`, and `NEW` must come from real market evidence rather than presentation fill.
- Current RMT platform fee policy is `RMT_EXECUTION_V2` version 2: 25 basis points on gross input for atomic, independently admitted provider execution. Uniswap V3 V2 is public/live; Uniswap V2 V2 remains public-off pending a separate release. No hidden surcharge, buyback, or Distribution routing is part of the Token trade path.
- Each additional provider activation requires an explicit owner decision plus separately reviewed implementation, proof, release, and Production activation.
- Future owner-authorized ecosystem revenue may market-buy RMT. This is a future policy direction, not current execution authority.
- RMT-token fee economics remain a separate accounting domain from any future RMT platform fee.
- The Distribution Center is intended to return value to participating communities.
- Future NFT marketplace economics are intended to account for artists and creators as well as Distribution. NFT execution and those economics require separate authorization.
- Token/NFT Project Market relationships require owner confirmation and independent technical verification. Names, symbols, branding, metadata, contract functions, access gates, or social accounts do not establish a relationship.
- RMT is noncustodial. Users retain wallet signing authority; RMT does not hold user keys.

## Independent product authorities

The following are deliberately distinct:

```text
MARKET_VISIBILITY
!= CURATED_MARKET_AUTHORITY
!= EXECUTION_ELIGIBILITY
!= PROJECT_RELATIONSHIP
!= DISTRIBUTION_ELIGIBILITY
```

A visible market can remain read-only. An executable route must be independently proven by the existing provider-specific verification stack. A successful trade or mint never auto-admits a project or creates a cross-asset relationship.

## Owner-undecided economics

The owner has not decided:

- the exact future platform-fee percentage;
- the percentage of revenue allocated to buybacks;
- buyback cadence;
- Distribution weights;
- creator or artist splits; or
- a future fee-activation date.

These unresolved choices authorize no implementation, deployment, collection, routing, or production configuration change.
