## Contracts and authority

| Boundary | Who can act, and what can change |
| --- | --- |
| Bootstrap, factory and fixed token | The deployer fixes chain, dependency code hashes, guard, registry, reviewed V2 deployer, creation fee and recipient bindings. The directory must publish the exact factory runtime before canonical registry admission. The creator selects one of three fixed supplies and an optional project treasury share, commits metadata and pays the fixed creation fee. Supply mints once. Creation receives zero backing. Overpayment refund failure rolls back the transaction. Anyone can release accrued creation fees to the immutable operating treasury. There is no caller-supplied payee. |
| Curve and curve fee escrow | A trader authorizes a bounded buy or sell with a deadline and protected output. The curve holds sold-token state, real backing and unsold inventory. Fee accrual is confined to its own ledger and immutable beneficiaries claim their own credit. A successor gains accrual permission after a settled same-token migration. Existing fee balances and entitlements stay in the original ledger. |
| Migration and admission | The creator prepares/proposes an exact adjacent successor. Two of three directory EOAs authorize the registry digest. Public execution after the two-day delay is bounded by a seven-day window and the signed deadline. Registry consumption precedes the asset-bearing factory call; it advances the current pointer and history after settlement checks. The same token, balances, economic commitments and original ledger survive. A failed execution rolls back. A creator may cancel and an expired proposal may be cleared. The initial V2 deployer is pinned; V3 and later adjacent templates require a separately reviewed runtime and delayed directory publication. Version admission is bounded at 32. |
| Emergency and key continuity | Two distinct directory EOAs may immediately suspend registry/discovery and migration admission, or cancel an exact pending directory action. Resumption requires delayed directory authorization. Existing active curve trades, LP principal custody and fee claims remain reachable. An application-side shutdown is a separate control. Signer addresses are immutable; there is no onchain rotation, rescue authority or one-signer fallback. Losing two keys can strand governance operations. Compromise of two keys threatens factory/template admission and migration review assumptions. The creator and settlement checks still apply to a project's migration. |
| Graduation and LP custody | The authorized graduation engine alone can initialize the intended guarded PoolKey. At the exact threshold, verified assets fund and settle atomic full-range v4 minting under permanent engine custody. Public graduation retries are bounded by explicit deadlines and failed settlement rolls the transaction back. The guard enables only `beforeInitialize`. There is no position transfer, principal withdrawal, arbitrary call or administrative upgrade in this path. |
| LP fees and external trading | Any caller can ask the engine to collect only fees earned by the original position, using a zero-liquidity update and the fixed per-launch ledger. Verify owner, full PoolKey, range, permissions and unchanged liquidity. Native and token credits are separate; each recipient claims their balance. Independent v4 routers and outside LP positions are permitted. RMT earns only its committed share of realized fees from its original position. |

Review the five AST roots (bootstrap, directory, registry and V2/V3 successor deployers) and their entire closure. Review the external execution of the live pinned PoolManager, PositionManager and Permit2 and their mutable protocol controls. Include the Router 2.1.2, Quoter and StateView bindings and their effect on receipt and recovery validation. Test recipient behavior and cross-contract callbacks. Historical direct-launch V1/V2 contracts, the existing RMT token/curve and standalone replacement-token escrow remain separate. The escrow is outside the reachable reviewed template set. Admission of a future successor requires its own review.

## Decisions for the independent reviewer

1. Disposition each high and the migration warnings with the concrete deployed trust and callback graph. Include token/native funding, repayment ceilings, unsolicited asset behavior, reentrancy across ledgers, exact accounting and whether any candidate fix would change cancellation, claims or rollback.
2. Verify the before-initialize hook cannot admit an alternative initializer for the canonical full PoolKey. Assess the fixed tick math, opening price/rounding bounds, exact settlement and custody verifications, and liveness near the threshold. Inspect alternative pools as separate markets and price competition from outside LPs.
3. Validate constructor and immutable binding checks, exact latest deployment dependency identities, and migration candidate/admission policy. Assess conflicting proposals, two-key compromise or loss, signer independence, runtime pins versus behavior, and whether governance recovery meets the required operating model.
4. Verify fee-entitlement isolation from backing and position principal, receive/refund behavior for fixed-contract payees and consequences of unavailable recipients. Validate proposed parameters separately from implementation behavior.
5. Review what chain and transaction evidence can truthfully publish. Require exact-address/token executable provenance, canonical full PoolKey, receipt and trace recovery, persistent index replay and L1 verification before the corresponding release decision. Physical-wallet evidence, hosted exact-pool routing and indexer coverage remain separate acceptance gates.
