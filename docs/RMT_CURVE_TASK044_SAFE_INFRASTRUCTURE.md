## Preparation and verification

The official factory's `createProxyWithNonce` derives the CREATE2 salt from the initializer hash and a `uint256` nonce. `createChainSpecificProxyWithNonce` adds the chain ID to that packed salt. The CREATE2 initcode combines the factory's `proxyCreationCode()` and the singleton address padded to 32 bytes. The factory, singleton, initializer, nonce and method must all match the intended calculation. Use distinct owner-approved configurations and salts for Operations and Reserve. An `eth_call` of the ordinary factory method verified the locally derived address for a synthetic policy with both versions. The synthetic predictions were checked as empty at the observation block. They do not bind any RMT payee. [Safe deployment reference](https://docs.safe.global/reference-smart-account/deployment/SafeProxyFactory) · [1.4.1 factory source](https://github.com/safe-global/safe-smart-account/blob/v1.4.1/contracts/proxies/SafeProxyFactory.sol) · [1.5.0 factory source](https://github.com/safe-global/safe-smart-account/blob/v1.5.0/contracts/proxies/SafeProxyFactory.sol)

Before binding either immutable fee recipient, verify its deployed proxy, singleton, initialized owner list and threshold, fallback handler, and absence of unexpected modules or guards. Rehearse native and ERC-20 receipt, owner-authorized claims and an outgoing transfer using the exact Safe configuration on an isolated fork. Check that Operations and Reserve resolve to distinct initialized contracts. A change to spending permissions inside the external Safe preserves its address and requires the Safe's own quorum. RMT's project ledger and factory do not redirect immutable payouts. An undeployed prediction or a successful version probe is insufficient to bind a paying contract.

The Safe sends an ordinary zero-value call directly to the applicable RMT ledger. The curve ETH ledger uses `claim()`. The LP ledger uses `claim()` for both assets or `claimAsset(true)` for ETH and `claimAsset(false)` for ERC-20. A canonical receipt must identify both the Safe execution and the ledger beneficiary. Keep the proposal's Safe transaction hash separate from the mined transaction hash and cross-check actual native and token settlement. Operations creation fees can be pushed to the compatible receiving Safe. Preserve separate Operations and Reserve history, balance and outgoing approval records.
