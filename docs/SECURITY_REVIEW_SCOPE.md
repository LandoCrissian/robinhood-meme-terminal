# RMT V6 independent security review scope

## Objective

Assess the live policy-driven V6 deployment, reconstruct the release from repository source and receipts, verify deployed bytecode and immutable configuration, and review operational controls while public creation is intentionally paused for V7 preparation. The canonical deployment record is [MAINNET_V6_DEPLOYMENT.md](MAINNET_V6_DEPLOYMENT.md). Existing V5 launches are immutable and are in scope only where V6 depends on legacy identity lookup.

## System boundaries

- fixed-supply token clone and initialization
- V6 factory, identity reservation, and one-time official RMT migration
- append-only policy registration, availability, and default selection
- Fair and Open bonding-curve markets
- native and token fee accounting through the immutable splitter
- permanent market/adapter fee-source bindings and rejection of transfers that are not explicit fee deposits
- graduation trigger and one-time migration
- Uniswap V4 hook, pool reservation, settlement, full-range locked position, and fee realization
- launch gate, fresh `RMTV6Governance` acting as both protocol authority and fee-holding treasury, and fresh `VersionedFactoryRegistry` governed by it and initialized to V5; no legacy governance/registry dependency
- phased browser deployment console, generated bytecode artifacts, and fork-rehearsal-only Foundry path
- V6 event ingestion and historical economics boundary

## Required threat analysis

- unauthorized minting, inventory withdrawal, reserve theft, or fee redirection
- reentrancy and callback behavior across PoolManager, token, splitter, creator, and treasury
- reserve insolvency, rounding extraction, dust cycles, target crossing, and slippage
- Fair Start bypass, sybil limitations, sandwiching, MEV, and same-block behavior
- clone reinitialization and identity/migration front-running
- policy mutation, bypass of the registry's canonical market/adapter locks, implementation substitution, or historical-economics misrepresentation
- graduation replay, partial settlement, stuck assets, wrong pool, and price discontinuity
- liquidity-principal removal through direct, indirect, governance, or callback paths
- permissionless fee collection changing principal or redirecting native/token proceeds
- seller proceeds, overpayment refunds, forced assets, direct gifts, or V4 donations masquerading as trading fees
- signer-authorized delayed creator-payout proposals, permissionless exact-call relay, allowed-destination restriction, evidence hash, replay nonce, governance-approved treasury invalidation, and ownership of deferred balances
- preservation of permanent original-creator identity while the current fee recipient changes
- complete reconstruction of the single V6 governance history, including fresh-registry activation; no unrecognized, unmatched, stale-epoch, cancelled, or expired proposal may remain executable
- outside-liquidity and donation rejection, and proof that the locked adapter position receives every canonical-pool LP fee
- redirect/collection ordering, including fees accrued before but collected after an accepted redirect
- prospective-signer proof of control; expiring acceptance bound to the current epoch, exact action, affected signer, and threshold; candidate-controlled revocation before execution; acceptance scoping, staleness, consumption, replay resistance, and the 1-of-1 to 2-of-2 liveness boundary
- atomic signer addition/removal/replacement with threshold changes; configuration-epoch invalidation, proposal inspection, ordering, cancellation, expiry, replay, and permissionless delayed execution
- guardian privilege and launch-entry-point pause bypass
- inactive/stale factory launch attempts and the exact one-time paused official-migration exception
- proof that the official V6 launch creates a new token address/supply without copying, swapping, crediting, or migrating legacy holder balances, and that UI/release disclosures cannot imply otherwise
- premature gate opening while the official migration remains unconsumed
- version activation while configuration is incomplete or launch gate is open
- website artifact/source mismatch and stale deployment-state recovery
- denial of service and unbounded gas behavior

## Economic review

Independently simulate low activity, whales, repeated buy/sell cycles, target approach, target crossing, panic sells, rounding effects, post-graduation two-way Uniswap v4 trading, fee realization timing, payout redirects, and exact creator/protocol distributions in both ETH and the launched token. Assess whether the 2 ETH graduation target and virtual reserves create the disclosed approximately 17–18 ETH graduation FDV and whether the curve-to-pool price transition remains within the tested bound.
