Campaign controllers may admit only matching profiles, with a two-day notice and finite campaign-specific eligibility proofs. They cannot add new implementation profiles or substitute another sink. Future supported implementations require a separately reviewed version/factory; there is no unrestricted upgrade authority.

The sink argument depends on verified deployed EVM code and the NFT's actual authorization rules. [ERC-721](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-721) and [ERC-1155](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1155) define transfer/ownership interfaces, not universal irreversibility. [EIP-3607](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-3607) rejects ordinary senders with deployed code; [EIP-7702](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7702) does not permit replacing arbitrary deployed contract code through an EOA delegation authorization. The target chain's relevant behavior and exact sink runtime must be verified before release. Source review must independently establish that no reachable NFT or sink function can move, approve, seize, restore or upgrade accepted assets. This is finite engineering evidence, not a claim of mathematical irreversibility under arbitrary chain changes or cryptographic breaks.

## Rewards and funding

The intended initial $20 reserve and nominal $0.05 reward, potentially later $0.50, are goals, **not an on-chain USD valuation**. Real fixed RMT units and manual funding are unassigned in the proposed configuration. USD quoting rejects until an independently defensible actual RMT/USD policy is separately approved. The local example explicitly assumes 1 synthetic RMT = $0.01, manually funds 2,000 synthetic units and pays five units per eligible NFT quantity. It does not approve those token amounts in production.

Each successful surrender retires the exact NFT ID/quantity and pays the exact funded RMT reward atomically. Any authorization, retirement, supply/ownership, reward transfer or accounting failure reverses the whole transaction. That rollback is not a return feature for an accepted surrender. There are no IOUs, mints, automatic swaps/top-ups or use of creator fees, Operations, Reserve, project treasuries, royalties or locked LP principal. Integer units have 18 decimals; insufficient reserves and dust cannot satisfy a larger reward. Network gas is separate and may exceed the reward's economic value.

`funded + unsolicited = reserve + paid + recovered`; outstanding accepted-but-unpaid liability is always zero because acceptance and payout are atomic. Direct gifts are recorded separately from manual funding. Only remaining uncommitted RMT may be recovered after close and a fresh bounded notice, to the campaign controller. No accepted NFT can be recovered by anyone.

## Eligibility, controls and anti-abuse

Proofs bind chain, canonical factory/campaign, collection/runtime, token standard, permanent-retirement mode, policy commitment, token ID and maximum quantity. Each transaction binds claimant nonce, policy epoch, expected reward and deadline. Eight sorted unique IDs/twenty units bound a batch; proofs have at most 32 siblings. Wallet, wallet/collection, collection and per-ID lifetime caps remain consumed forever. There is no reset after rate changes, campaign close, controller changes or recipient decisions. Wallet caps alone are not Sybil resistance: finite reviewed NFT eligibility and fixed original supply are essential. Counterfeit metadata, self-minting an unapproved collection, social actions and Firebase records confer no eligibility or ownership.

The controller can pause immediately. Collection/rate/close/recovery/controller changes require the two-day notice and bounded execution window; current epochs and incoming-controller acceptance apply. Rate changes concern future accepted surrenders. An outstanding signed request carries its original policy/reward/deadline and must revert on conflicting terms. Controller changes cannot redirect already settled rewards or extract NFTs. Loss of controller authority may prevent reserve administration; it never creates an unauthorized recovery override.

Canonical reorgs can orphan a surrender and reverse its state before finality. The indexer must roll back its observation and retain the original wallet journal without automatic resubmission. Local two-block confirmations and corroborated L2 state do not establish Ethereum L1 finality. Independent settlement currently requires a single-transaction fixture block; unproven multiple-transaction historical balance attribution stays unavailable.
