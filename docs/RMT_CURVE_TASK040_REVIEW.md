## Review perimeter

- **Assets and roles.** Before graduation the curve owns real ETH backing and unsold fixed-supply inventory. The factory holds its separate creation-fee credit. The revenue ledger holds beneficiary credits. During graduation the engine, Permit2 and PositionManager briefly handle finite approvals and exact transfers from the original curve. After graduation the original position NFT resides with the graduation engine. Its fee ledger owns only collected proceeds. Independent LPs, swap callers and fee beneficiaries are different roles.
- **Authority.** The directory pins exact factory and adjacent-version template identities. Project migration also requires creator assent, two distinct EOA signatures, an exact digest including the deployment domain/nonce and a public timelock. Governance can suspend canonical registration and migration, and existing trades and claims remain available. The reviewed system lacks arbitrary administration, principal withdrawal and mutable recipient policy. Signer continuity, independence and loss of quorum are release decisions.
- **Evidence.** Runtime hashes verify deployed identity against expected bytes. Behavior still depends on external protocol code, upstream mutable configuration and the actual callback graph. Fork and fixture evidence describe specific executions. RPC observations require a canonical hash and exact token, factory and full PoolKey. L2 observations keep L1 finality unverified until separate Ethereum-backed validation is available.
